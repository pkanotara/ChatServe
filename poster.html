About the Project

ChatServe is a multi-tenant SaaS platform that empowers small and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs) to automate their day-to-day business operations entirely through WhatsApp.

It eliminates the need for dedicated apps or technical staff by offering automated chatbot flows, catalog management, order handling, and customer communication — all within WhatsApp.

Abstract

MSMEs in India depend heavily on WhatsApp for sales and customer interaction, yet most lack affordable automation tools. ChatServe addresses this by delivering a WhatsApp Cloud API-powered SaaS system where businesses can onboard via a chatbot, publish catalogs, and manage orders without human intervention. The platform supports multi-tenant isolation, real-time notifications, and an admin dashboard. Designed with simplicity and low cost at its core, ChatServe reduces operational overhead and improves customer response time significantly.

Problem Context
  • Over 60 million MSMEs in India rely on WhatsApp for business communication
  • Manual order taking leads to errors, delays & customer drop-off
  • No 24×7 availability for customer queries or order placement
  • Existing enterprise solutions are too expensive for small businesses
  • No structured catalog or inventory management over WhatsApp
  • Business owners spend excessive time on repetitive messaging
Existing Systems & Limitations

WhatsApp Business App: Manual replies only, no automation, single device, no API access.

Enterprise API Platforms: Interakt, AiSensy, WATI, Infobip offer partial automation but require BSP partnerships, high monthly fees, and technical setup beyond MSME capability.

Key Gaps: No integrated onboarding chatbot; no built-in catalog + order management; no low-cost self-setup option.

Proposed System — ChatServe

ChatServe is a self-onboarding, multi-tenant SaaS platform built on the WhatsApp Cloud API. A business owner simply sends a WhatsApp message to the system — the chatbot guides them through onboarding, catalog setup, and configuration, requiring zero technical knowledge.

  • Automation: State-machine chatbot handles customer queries, orders, and confirmations autonomously
  • Simplicity: No app install required — operates entirely inside WhatsApp
  • Low Cost: Self-hosted or shared cloud — drastically cheaper than BSP platforms
  • Multi-tenant: Each business gets isolated data, its own catalog, and independent configuration

Logic

Fig. Architecture: The backend exposes REST APIs consumed by the React dashboard, while a dedicated webhook handler processes incoming WhatsApp messages through the state-machine chatbot engine.

Methodology
  • Chatbot Onboarding: Businesses register by messaging the platform number; a guided flow collects business name, type, catalog, and phone credentials
  • State-Based Engine: Each conversation is tracked via a finite-state machine stored in MongoDB; states persist across sessions
  • Multi-Tenant SaaS: Each registered business entity owns isolated collections; routing is performed via the incoming phone number
  • Webhook-Driven: WhatsApp Cloud API delivers all messages via HTTPS webhook; no polling required
  • Dashboard: React frontend provides catalog management, order tracking, and broadcast messaging for business owners

Key Features
  • Chatbot Automation: Fully automated customer interaction — no human agent needed
  • Self-Onboarding: Businesses configure themselves through guided WhatsApp conversation
  • Dashboard: Real-time order tracking, catalog CRUD, and analytics
  • Broadcast Messaging: Business can send promotional messages to all customers
  • Auto Webhook Setup: System auto-registers the business's WhatsApp webhook on onboarding

  • Order Notifications: Instant WhatsApp alerts to business on new orders

Conclusion

ChatServe successfully demonstrates that WhatsApp — already ubiquitous among Indian MSMEs — can serve as a powerful business automation channel without requiring any additional infrastructure. The platform's self-onboarding design, state-machine chatbot engine, and multi-tenant architecture collectively lower the barrier for small businesses to adopt digital automation. The system is cost-effective, scalable, and immediately deployable — making it a practical solution for the underserved MSME segment.
